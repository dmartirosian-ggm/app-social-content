## Week two: tiny habit of the day (one clip a day)

**Day 8: Put on shoes** (`08-put-on-shoes.mp4`)
Our shoes always go in the same spot by the front door. When it's time to leave, nobody has to hunt for them, and putting them on without help earns a star.

Spark is a free chore and reward chart for parents of kids ages 2 to 6. It's on the App Store.
#toddlerroutine #morningroutine #rewardchart #toddlermom

**Day 9: Bath time** (`09-bath-time.mp4`)
Bath happens at the same time every night, and they get to pick one toy to bring in. Knowing what comes next makes bedtime a lot smoother.

Spark is a free chore and reward chart for parents of kids ages 2 to 6. It's on the App Store.
#bathtime #bedtimeroutine #toddlerroutine #momlife

**Day 10: Drink water** (`10-drink-water.mp4`)
We keep a cup of water where little hands can reach it. If it's empty by lunch, that's a star. How do you get your kids to drink more water?

#toddlerhealth #toddlerroutine #rewardchart #toddlermom

**Day 11: Pack backpack** (`11-pack-backpack.mp4`)
The backpack gets packed after dinner, not in the morning rush. It's one less thing to do before school, and they're proud to do it themselves.

**Day 12: Say please and thank you** (`12-say-please.mp4`)
